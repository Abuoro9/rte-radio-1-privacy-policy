Introduction

RTE Radio 1 Live Ireland is a radio streaming application designed to let you listen to live radio over the internet. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the application.

The application does not require you to create an account or sign in to listen. By using the application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

▤

Information We Collect

RTE Radio 1 Live Ireland does not directly ask you to provide your name, password, telephone number, or other personal information to use its core radio streaming features.

However, third-party services integrated into the application may automatically collect certain information. Depending on the services used and your device settings, this may include device information, advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic data, approximate location, and information about advertisements shown or interacted with.

▣

Advertising

RTE Radio 1 Live Ireland uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the application.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses, and information about advertising interactions to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and, where permitted, personalize advertisements.

The information processed and the advertising personalization available to you may depend on your location, consent choices, device settings, and applicable privacy requirements.

For more information about how Google handles data in advertising products, review Google's Privacy Policy and information about Google's advertising technologies .

⚙

Advertising Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and the advertising services configured in the application, privacy options may be available to manage your advertising consent or personalization preferences.

You may also be able to manage advertising personalization through your Android device settings or the relevant Google privacy controls. Changing these settings may affect the advertisements you see, but it does not necessarily remove all advertisements.

Where a consent or privacy-options form is required, the available choices depend on the services configured in the application and applicable requirements.

▥

Analytics and Diagnostics

The application may use Firebase Analytics to understand how the application is used and to help improve its features, performance, and reliability.

If enabled and configured in the application, analytics services may process information such as app usage events, device information, approximate location, and diagnostic or performance information.

The information collected depends on the analytics features enabled and the applicable privacy settings. For more information, see Firebase's privacy information .

♫

Live Audio Streaming

RTE Radio 1 Live Ireland connects to audio streaming servers to provide live radio playback.

When you listen, your device communicates with the relevant streaming servers. The streaming provider may process technical connection information, including your IP address and connection details, to deliver the audio stream and maintain service reliability.

Audio streaming requires an internet connection and may use mobile data or Wi-Fi depending on your device connection.

⌁

Internet and Network Information

An internet connection is required to stream live radio, load advertisements, and access online features provided by the application.

The application may check network connectivity to determine whether a connection is available and to manage streaming or reconnection behavior.

Network connectivity checks are used to support the application's functionality. Your internet service provider may independently process connection information under its own privacy practices.

▤

Local Storage

The application may store settings and preferences locally on your device, such as your selected theme or other application preferences.

These settings help the application remember your preferences between sessions. The application does not require an account to store these settings.

Locally stored preferences may remain on your device until they are cleared, the application data is deleted, or the application is uninstalled.

↗

Third-Party Services

The application may rely on third-party providers for advertising, analytics, audio streaming, and related technical services.

These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services and in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

Examples of relevant providers may include Google AdMob, Firebase, and the provider operating the audio stream. The specific services used may change as the application is updated.

⇧

How Information Is Shared

The application does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use its core listening features.

Information may be processed by third-party providers when necessary to deliver advertisements, measure application usage, provide audio streaming, maintain security, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Those providers may handle information under their own privacy policies. Their processing practices may differ depending on your location and the services being used.

⌫

Data Retention and Deletion

Application preferences stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the application data or uninstalling the application.

Information collected by third-party providers is retained and deleted according to their respective policies, settings, and applicable legal requirements. The application developer does not control all retention practices of external providers.

♡

Children's Privacy

RTE Radio 1 Live Ireland is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

Third-party services integrated into the application may have their own age-related requirements and data-handling practices. Parents and guardians should review the applicable service policies and device privacy settings.

If you have a privacy concern relating to the application, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the app's Google Play Store listing.

♙

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to support the security and reliability of the application.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Third-party providers are responsible for the security measures applicable to the services they operate.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Where information is processed by a third-party provider, you may need to exercise applicable rights directly with that provider. You may also use any privacy or advertising controls made available within the application or on your device.

◷

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

The revised policy will be made available within the application and, where applicable, through the privacy policy link on the Google Play Store listing. Please review it periodically.

Contact Us
